Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to switch lanes, swipe up to jump and swipe down to slide. Jump over low obstacles like hot dogs, slide under bars like churros, and dodge big ones like pancake stacks and rolling donuts. Collect donuts along the way!

Who is Hungry Hank?

Hank is a very hungry kid chasing your food. If you bump into something, he catches up and takes a bite – you keep running with a bite mark. Bump into something again while he's still right behind you and he'll eat you in one bite. Game over!

How do I unlock new characters?

The donuts you collect are saved to your wallet. Open the Character Shop from the main menu to buy and select characters: Pizza Pete, Big Bun, Nigiri Nik, Kiki Cupcake, Taco Tito and Scoops.

What are the worlds?

The further you run, the more worlds you visit: Sweet Street, Fruit Farm, Sushi Bay and Choco Canyon. The game also gets faster as you go.

I can't hear the music.

Music plays only during a run. Make sure your iPhone isn't on silent mode (check the ring/silent switch or Action button), your volume is up, and the sound button in the top corner of the game shows 🔊.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Your donuts, characters and best score are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them, and they can't be restored.

Does Munch Run need internet?

No. The game works fully offline.

Are there in-app purchases or ads?

No. Everything in the game is unlocked with donuts you collect while playing.
